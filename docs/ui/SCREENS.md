# Screens

## Prospects

The acquisitions desk opens here. The eye lands on the four counts, then the call list, then the park table beside the map. The decision is who to call this week.

## Park

One community. The eye lands on the name and score, the call line, then the loan. The decision is whether the pressure is real enough to log a call.

## Rate desk

Six figures first: 5-year, 10-year, SOFR, prime, Arizona unemployment, and the CMBS spread. The rest of the series is behind “All series.” The decision is what a refinance costs today.

## Signals

One tab at a time: FHA, Form D, filings, distress, sales, owners. The decision is whether a filing or a sale changes the call.

## Markets

Arizona counties ranked by parks on the desk. The decision is where the book is concentrated. It does not show rent or population.

## Owners

One row per owner record, with the records that hold the most parks first. A Same name column shows other parks that use that recorded name. It is not a finding that one person controls them, and it is not a beneficial-owner chart.

## Pipeline

Deals already saved, plus the buy box. The decision is what is due today.

## Alerts, portfolio, sources

The same tables as before, in the new shell. Sources is the freshness log.
